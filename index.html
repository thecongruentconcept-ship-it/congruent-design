The Congruent Method

Every project follows the same five steps, from the first site visit to the years after handover.

  1. Assess

    The site as it is: sun, soil, slope, water, views and how it will be used.

  2. Align

    Scope, budget and priorities agreed before design begins.

  3. Design

    One complete plan: planting, hardscape, grading, lighting and irrigation.

  4. Execute

    Built by licensed contractors, with Congruent confirming the work matches the plan.

  5. Steward

    After handover, we assess the landscape and direct the care it needs.

Root zone wetted

For established plants on pressure-compensating drip, 90% efficient. Each emitter wets a circle of soil about 3 feet across in sandy fill, 3.5 feet in sandy loam and 4 feet in loam, and only wetted soil stores water for the plant. Water is applied when half of that stored water is used. Weather: typical daily reference ET for the Lower Colorado River desert (CIMIS Zone 18); live readings, when available, come from the AZMET Fort Mohave station. Plant water factors from WUCOLS Low Desert ratings, calibrated in the field. New plantings need more frequent water until established.
