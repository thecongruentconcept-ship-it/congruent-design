How a project goes

  1. Property walk

“I left golf because of how much water it used. Now I design yards that use only what they need.”

Mike Brenneman founded Congruent Concepts after three decades building golf courses and landscapes: underground irrigation, grading and drainage, and the budgets and crews that hold a project together.

He grew up outdoors in Wadena, Minnesota, and now works where water matters most. Every Congruent yard is designed around the desert it sits in, with plants chosen for Havasu heat and watering set by the numbers rather than by habit.

Design, manage, assess, solve: one person accountable from the first walk to the fifth summer.
